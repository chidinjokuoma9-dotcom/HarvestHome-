Discover houses, land, products and farm produce on HarvestHome

HarvestHome is an online marketplace designed to help people discover offers and connect with sellers. You can explore listings for houses and other property, land, products, and farm produce, then contact the person who posted an offer to ask questions and confirm the details. Listings, prices and availability can change, so check the current information before making a decision.

Houses and property

Compare the information provided about a property's location, price, condition and sale or lease terms. Before paying or signing anything, arrange a suitable viewing and independently check the owner's identity, authority to transact, title documents and any relevant local requirements.

Land

Ask about the precise location, boundaries, access and intended use of a land listing. Do not rely on photographs or messages alone. Have title and survey details checked by qualified local professionals and appropriate authorities before committing funds.

Products

Read the product description and ask about specifications, condition, quantity, price, availability, collection and delivery. For higher-value purchases, agree in writing how the item will be inspected and handed over.

Farm produce

Buyers and sellers should confirm produce type, grade or quality, quantity, packaging, timing, transport and total costs. For bulk orders, agree how the quantity and quality will be checked at handover.

How to use the marketplace

1. Explore and compare

Use the marketplace categories and available filters to find relevant offers. Read the full description, check the location and compare important terms rather than relying on a headline or photo alone.

2. Ask the seller

Contact the listing owner to confirm availability, ask questions and clarify price, viewing, collection or delivery arrangements. Keep records of important messages and agreed terms.

3. Verify before paying

Make independent checks appropriate to the transaction. Be cautious of pressure to pay urgently, unexplained extra fees, or requests to send money to an unrelated person. Keep receipts and payment records.

4. Publish an accurate listing

Sellers should post genuine photos and truthful descriptions, provide a clear price and location, and offer only property or goods they are authorised to sell or lease. Respond honestly to buyer questions and update or remove unavailable offers.

Browsing HarvestHome from outside Nigeria

People in Nigeria and abroad can visit HarvestHome to explore listings connected to Nigeria. Accessing a listing from another country does not mean the seller offers international delivery or that every payment method supports cross-border transactions. Before committing, confirm the seller can serve your location, the accepted currency and payment method, delivery or collection, documentation, total cost and any applicable legal or customs requirements.

Safety reminder: HarvestHome helps people discover listings and contact other users; it does not guarantee a listing's ownership, authenticity, quality, legality or transaction outcome. A profile, badge or moderation decision is not a substitute for independent checks.

Frequently asked questions

Does a listing guarantee that an item or property is still available?

No. Availability and terms can change. Contact the seller to confirm the current status before arranging a viewing, delivery or payment.

Can I safely pay based only on photos or messages?

No. Photos and messages are not enough to establish ownership, condition or legitimacy. Verify the seller and relevant details independently, and use written terms appropriate to the transaction.

Does browsing from abroad mean international delivery is available?

No. International visitors can explore listings, but delivery, payment, currency and regulatory arrangements depend on the specific seller and transaction. Confirm them directly before paying.

How can I report a concern or ask HarvestHome a question?

Email harvesthcn@gmail.com and include the relevant listing link or details. Do not include passwords, PINs or one-time verification codes.
