How to Buy and Sell Safely on HarvestHome in Nigeria

Practical guidance for buyers and sellers of houses, land, products and farm produce.

Online marketplaces make it easier to discover opportunities, but every buyer and seller should check important details before committing money or goods. Use this guide as a starting point, and make independent checks appropriate to the transaction.

1. Find and review a listing

  • Start at the HarvestHome marketplace and use the available categories, location filters and search tools to narrow your options.
  • Read the full description, price, location and sale or lease terms. Check that photos and videos appear consistent with the description.
  • Be cautious if information is missing, contradictory, unusually urgent or too good to be true. Ask clear questions before arranging a meeting or payment.
  • Save the listing link so you can compare details and refer back to the same offer.

2. Contact the seller carefully

  • Ask for the details you need to assess the offer, including availability, condition, exact location and any extra charges.
  • Keep records of important messages and agreed terms. Be wary of anyone who pressures you to rush or refuses reasonable questions.
  • Do not share passwords, one-time passcodes, bank PINs or unnecessary identity and financial information.
  • Do not assume a profile, badge, listing approval or friendly conversation proves that a person owns an item or has authority to sell it.

3. Check property and land before paying

  • For houses and land, visit the property where practical and verify the seller's identity and authority to sell or lease it.
  • Independently check title documents, survey details, boundaries, planning restrictions and other relevant records with qualified local professionals and the appropriate authorities.
  • Do not rely only on photographs, screenshots, copied documents or verbal promises. Arrange independent legal advice for significant property transactions.
  • Use a written agreement that clearly identifies the parties, property, price, payment terms and conditions.

4. Take care with products and farm produce

  • Ask about condition, quantity, quality, specifications, delivery arrangements, returns and who pays transport costs.
  • Where practical, inspect the goods or request credible, current evidence before committing to a large order.
  • For bulk produce or high-value products, agree on how quantity and quality will be checked at handover and keep written records.
  • Meet in a sensible, public or otherwise appropriate location when exchanging goods, and consider bringing someone you trust.

5. Protect your money

  • Do not send deposits or full payment until you have made checks appropriate to the transaction and understand the written terms.
  • Be cautious about last-minute changes to bank details, payment to unrelated third parties, unusual payment methods or requests to pay extra fees to “release” an item.
  • Confirm the recipient and transaction details independently. Keep receipts and proof of payment.
  • HarvestHome does not guarantee a transaction's ownership, authenticity, legality, quality or outcome. A listing or moderation status is not a substitute for your own checks.

6. If you are selling

  • Publish accurate descriptions, genuine photos and clear prices, locations and terms. Only offer items or property you are authorised to sell or lease.
  • Do not post another person's private information or misleading documents. Respond honestly to reasonable buyer questions.
  • Confirm that payment has actually arrived in your account before handing over goods or access. Do not rely on screenshots or messages claiming that a transfer is pending.
  • Keep records of the listing, conversations, agreed terms, delivery and payment.

7. Report suspicious listings or behaviour

If you notice suspected fraud, impersonation, misleading information, unsafe content or another rule violation, preserve the listing link and relevant evidence, then contact harvesthcn@gmail.com.

Read the HarvestHome Content & Safety Policy for more information about prohibited content and reporting. If you believe a crime has occurred or someone is in immediate danger, contact the appropriate local authorities.

Remember: These tips are general safety information, not legal, financial or property-specific advice. The checks needed depend on the transaction. Take your time, verify independently and do not proceed if something does not feel right.
