Explore Nigerian Listings from the United States

HarvestHome helps people discover marketplace listings connected to Nigeria, including houses and property, land, products and farm produce. If you are browsing from the United States, you can explore listings online and contact sellers to ask questions and confirm details.

Explore HarvestHome listings

What can you explore?

  • Houses and property: Review listing details and ask about location, ownership documentation, property condition and safe viewing arrangements.
  • Land: Independently verify title documents, survey details, boundaries and relevant Nigerian requirements with appropriate professionals and authorities before making a commitment.
  • Products: Ask sellers about specifications, condition, price, availability and whether shipping or collection can be arranged.
  • Farm produce: Confirm quantity, quality, timing, transport and any applicable export or import requirements directly with the seller.

Important information for US-based visitors

Website access from the United States does not mean every seller offers international shipping or that every payment method supports cross-border transactions. Before paying, confirm the seller can serve your location, the accepted currency and payment method, the total cost, delivery or collection arrangements, and any applicable customs, import or regulatory requirements.

For property or land, arrange independent checks and obtain suitable local professional advice. Do not rely only on photographs, messages or documents supplied by a seller.

Safer browsing and transactions

  • Verify the seller and important listing details independently.
  • Be cautious of pressure to pay urgently, suspiciously low prices, or requests to use unverified payment channels.
  • Agree on the full price, fees, delivery or viewing terms and responsibilities before committing.
  • Keep records of important communications and transaction agreements.
