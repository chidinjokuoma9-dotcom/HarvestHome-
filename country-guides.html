HarvestHome connects people with marketplace listings for houses, land, products and farm produce. These guides provide country-specific starting points for checking property, understanding produce-trading requirements and making safer transaction decisions.

Choose the country where the property or produce transaction takes place. A visitor's country may be different from the country where the listing is located. The rules of the property's location and the origin and destination of the goods are the ones that matter.
