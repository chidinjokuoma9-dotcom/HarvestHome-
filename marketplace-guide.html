Houses and property

Compare location, price, condition and sale or lease terms. Before paying or signing, arrange a viewing and independently check the owner's identity, authority to transact, title documents and relevant local requirements.

Land

Ask about the precise location, boundaries, access and intended use. Don't rely on photos or messages alone. Have title and survey details checked by qualified local professionals and appropriate authorities before committing funds.

Products

Check specifications, condition, quantity, price, availability, collection and delivery. For higher-value purchases, agree in writing how the item will be inspected and handed over.

🌾

Farm produce

Confirm produce type, grade or quality, quantity, packaging, timing, transport and total costs. For bulk orders, agree how quantity and quality will be checked at handover.

The playbook

How to use the marketplace

Four simple steps to help you browse with confidence.

01

Explore & compare

Use categories and filters to find offers. Read the full description, check the location and compare key terms rather than relying on a headline or photo alone.

02

Ask the seller

Confirm availability, price, viewing, collection or delivery. Keep records of important messages and agreed terms.

03

Verify before paying

Make independent checks. Be careful with urgent payment pressure, unexplained fees or requests to pay an unrelated person. Keep receipts and payment records.

04

Post accurately

Sellers should use genuine photos and truthful descriptions, provide a clear price and location, and offer only property or goods they are authorised to sell or lease.

Across borders

Browsing HarvestHome from outside Nigeria

People in Nigeria and abroad can visit HarvestHome to explore listings connected to Nigeria. Accessing a listing from another country does not mean the seller offers international delivery or that every payment method supports cross-border transactions. Before committing, confirm that the seller can serve your location, the accepted currency and payment method, delivery or collection, documentation, total cost and any applicable legal or customs requirements.

🛡️ Safety reminder

HarvestHome helps people discover listings and contact other users; it does not guarantee a listing's ownership, authenticity, quality, legality or transaction outcome. A profile, badge or moderation decision is not a substitute for independent checks.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Does a listing guarantee that an item or property is still available?

No. Availability and terms can change. Contact the seller to confirm the current status before arranging a viewing, delivery or payment.

Can I safely pay based only on photos or messages?

No. Photos and messages are not enough to establish ownership, condition or legitimacy. Verify the seller and relevant details independently, and use written terms appropriate to the transaction.

Does browsing from abroad mean international delivery is available?

No. International visitors can explore listings, but delivery, payment, currency and regulatory arrangements depend on the specific seller and transaction. Confirm them directly before paying.

How can I report a concern or ask HarvestHome a question?

Email support@harvesthome.ng and include the relevant listing link or details. Do not include passwords, PINs or one-time verification codes.
