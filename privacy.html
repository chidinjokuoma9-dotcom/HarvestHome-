🌿 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HarvestHome collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use our global marketplace and News & Insights section.

Information we collect

We may collect account information such as your name and email address; seller profile information; listing details, photos and videos; messages and enquiries; payment and transaction references; and technical information needed to operate and secure the website.

News & Insights and submitted material

When you read News & Insights, we may process basic technical and usage information needed to deliver the pages, protect the service and understand readership. If you submit an article, tip, correction request or other material for consideration, we may use the contact details and information you provide to review the submission, communicate with you and, where accepted, prepare and publish the material. Published content may display your chosen byline, supplied biography, images or source details as part of the article. Please do not include personal information about yourself or another person unless it is necessary and you have a lawful basis to provide it.

Advertising and Google advertising cookies

If HarvestHome uses Google AdSense or other third-party advertising services, those providers may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies to serve, measure and improve advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to HarvestHome or other websites. Users may control personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. For information about how Google uses data when you use partner sites and apps, please review How Google uses data when you use our partners’ sites or apps. Other advertising vendors or networks may have their own privacy and opt-out options, which will be disclosed where applicable.

Seller reviews, replies and reports

If you submit a seller review, we process your account identifier, display name, star rating, written feedback and related seller/chat eligibility information to validate, moderate and display the review. Once approved, the review and chosen display name may be publicly visible on HarvestHome. A seller may publish a response. If you report a review, we process the report reason and account identifier so our moderation team can investigate and keep a record of the action. Review submissions may remain pending until reviewed, and hidden or reported material may be retained as reasonably necessary for safety, disputes and moderation records.

News & Insights comments and reports

If you submit a comment or reply on a published article, we process your account identifier, display name, comment text, article reference and submission time to provide and display the contribution publicly. Readers may report comments they believe are inappropriate. Admins and authorised Moderators can review reports and remove inappropriate comments from public view. Hidden comments and reports may be retained as reasonably necessary for moderation, safety, disputes and legal obligations.

How we use information

We use information to manage accounts and listings, connect buyers and sellers, provide messaging and enquiries, process marketplace payments, provide seller services and rewards, prevent fraud and abuse, moderate content, maintain security, improve the service and respond to complaints.

Payments and service providers

Payments may be processed by Paystack or another disclosed provider. HarvestHome does not need your card PIN, one-time password or full payment credentials. Information may be shared with hosting, authentication, storage, payment, security and analytics providers where necessary to operate the service.

Cookies and similar technologies

HarvestHome may use browser storage, cookies or similar technologies for sessions, preferences, security, analytics and service functionality. The News & Insights section may use a random browser identifier and a temporary tab-session identifier to estimate article readership and prevent repeated refreshes in the same tab session from inflating view counts. These identifiers are not designed to identify you by name, and article-view analytics do not store your IP address. If advertising is enabled, advertising partners may use technologies permitted by applicable policies and law.

Security, retention and your choices

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Information may be retained as reasonably necessary for service operation, security, disputes, legal obligations and legitimate business records. For privacy questions or correction requests, contact support@harvesthome.ng.

Children and minimum age (18+)

HarvestHome accounts and account-based marketplace services are intended only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly invite or permit anyone under 18 to create or use an account, publish or respond to listings, contact users through an account, conduct marketplace transactions, or submit account-based comments or other content. We ask users to confirm that they are at least 18 when creating an account. This is a self-declaration; it does not mean that HarvestHome currently performs independent identity or age-document verification for every user.

If we become aware, or have reasonable grounds to believe, that an account is being used by someone under 18, we may restrict or close the account and take appropriate steps to identify and delete personal information collected from that person, unless retention is required or permitted by applicable law for security, dispute resolution or legal compliance. We may retain a limited record of the report and action taken where reasonably necessary for those purposes. A parent or guardian who believes a person under 18 has provided personal information to HarvestHome may contact support@harvesthome.ng so we can review the matter.

We do not knowingly sell or use a child’s personal information for targeted advertising. Where information is found to relate to a child, we will assess the appropriate handling in accordance with applicable data-protection law.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as the service, legal requirements or advertising technology changes. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this policy.
