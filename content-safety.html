🌿 Content & Safety Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

HarvestHome is a user-generated marketplace. Sellers and buyers are responsible for keeping their contributions lawful, truthful and safe.

Allowed content

Listings should describe genuine houses, land, products, farm produce, services or other marketplace opportunities that the seller is authorised to offer. Photos, videos and descriptions should accurately represent the listing.

Prohibited content

Do not post scams, impersonation, stolen or counterfeit goods, unlawful property offers, dangerous or prohibited goods, fraudulent documents, misleading claims, abusive material, threats, harassment, malware, spam, fake reviews or content that infringes another person's rights.

Moderation

Free listings may become publicly visible immediately for 24 hours, but moderators can review, block, hide or remove listings that violate HarvestHome rules. Moderation does not guarantee accuracy, ownership, legality or value.

Reporting

Report suspected fraud, misleading listings, unsafe content or other violations to harvesthcn@gmail.com. Include the listing link or useful details where possible.
