Explore Nigerian Property, Products and Farm Produce from the UK

Living in the United Kingdom and interested in opportunities connected to Nigeria? HarvestHome is an online marketplace where you can explore listings for houses and property, land, products and farm produce. You can browse online and contact listing owners to ask questions and confirm details.

Explore HarvestHome listings

What can you explore?

  • Property and houses: Ask about location, viewing options, ownership documents and the process for any proposed purchase or lease.
  • Land: Independently verify title, survey information, boundaries and local legal requirements with qualified professionals in Nigeria.
  • Products: Check item condition, specifications, current price, availability and how collection or delivery would work.
  • Farm produce: Confirm quantity, quality, timing, packaging, transport and any relevant export or import requirements directly with the seller and appropriate authorities.

Before arranging a cross-border transaction

Being able to view a listing from the UK does not mean that a seller offers UK delivery or that a payment method supports international transactions. Contact the seller before committing and confirm the currency, total cost, payment arrangements, delivery or collection, documentation and any customs or regulatory requirements that may apply.

Do not rely on a listing or badge alone as proof of ownership, authenticity or legal status. Verify important information independently and be cautious of pressure to pay quickly.
