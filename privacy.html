🌿 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HarvestHome collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use our marketplace.

Information we collect

We may collect account information such as your name and email address; seller profile information; listing details, photos and videos; messages and enquiries; payment and transaction references; and technical information needed to operate and secure the website.

How we use information

We use information to manage accounts and listings, connect buyers and sellers, provide messaging and enquiries, process marketplace payments, provide seller services and rewards, prevent fraud and abuse, moderate content, maintain security, improve the service and respond to complaints.

Payments and service providers

Payments may be processed by Paystack or another disclosed provider. HarvestHome does not need your card PIN, one-time password or full payment credentials. Information may be shared with hosting, authentication, storage, payment, security and analytics providers where necessary to operate the service.

Cookies and similar technologies

HarvestHome may use browser storage, cookies or similar technologies for sessions, preferences, security, analytics and service functionality. If advertising is enabled, advertising partners may use technologies permitted by applicable policies and law.

Security, retention and your choices

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Information may be retained as reasonably necessary for service operation, security, disputes, legal obligations and legitimate business records. For privacy questions or correction requests, contact harvesthcn@gmail.com.

Children and updates

HarvestHome is not intended for children under 18 to create accounts or conduct marketplace transactions. We may update this policy as the service, legal requirements or advertising technology changes.
