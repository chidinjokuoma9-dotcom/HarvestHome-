Discover Nigerian Property, Products and Farm Produce

HarvestHome is an online marketplace that helps people discover listings and opportunities involving property, land, products and farm produce in Nigeria. Whether you are browsing from within Nigeria or from another country, you can explore available listings and contact sellers to learn more.

Explore HarvestHome listings

Explore marketplace categories

  • Houses and property: Browse property listings and ask sellers about location, ownership documentation, condition and viewing arrangements.
  • Land: Review available land listings and independently verify title documents, survey details, boundaries and relevant local requirements before making a commitment.
  • Products: Discover products listed by marketplace participants and ask about specifications, condition, price and availability.
  • Farm produce: Explore agricultural produce listings and confirm quantities, quality, harvest or delivery dates, and transport arrangements directly with the seller.

For visitors abroad, see our guides to exploring Nigerian listings from the United Kingdom, the United States and Canada.

Browsing from outside Nigeria

You can visit HarvestHome from abroad to explore listings connected to Nigeria. Before proceeding, contact the seller to confirm that the item is still available, whether they can serve your location, and what delivery, collection, documentation and payment arrangements are possible.

International access to the website does not mean every seller offers international delivery or that every payment method supports cross-border transactions. Confirm the full arrangement before sending money or sharing sensitive information.

Practical checks before a transaction

  • Verify the seller's identity and the listing details independently.
  • For property or land, seek appropriate local professional advice and check documents with the relevant authorities.
  • Agree on the total price, currency, delivery or viewing arrangements, and any additional fees before paying.
  • Be cautious of pressure to pay quickly, unusually low prices, or requests to send money through an unverified channel.
