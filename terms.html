6. HarvestHome rewards and referrals

HarvestHome may operate reward campaigns that recognize genuine seller participation and buyer referrals. Under the current reward guide, an eligible seller may earn 10 participation points for a qualifying listing, 5 recommendation points when a buyer recommends the seller through an approved listing, and 10 buyer-referral points when a verified unique buyer referral is recorded. A referred buyer must be genuine and unique; self-referrals, duplicate accounts, manipulation or fraudulent activity do not qualify.

The current qualification guide is 100 total points or 10 unique buyer referrals for Reward Qualified, and 250 total points or 25 unique buyer referrals for Gold level.
